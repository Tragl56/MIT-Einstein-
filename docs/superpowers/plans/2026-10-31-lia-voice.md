## Verification

- Explicit microphone start; real MediaRecorder and Web Audio using an oscillator connected to a synthetic MediaStream. Visual level changed from 0 to approximately 0.4.
- Stream tracks ended after stop and navigation. Permission granted after cancellation was stopped immediately; cancelled transcription was ignored and sent zero messages.
- Editable transcript preceded sending; backend confirmation remained required for contact requests.
- Synthesized playback events activated speaking visuals and could be interrupted. These events were simulated; real TTS acoustic amplitude and external transcription accuracy were not measured.
- Switching to text prevented a late voice reply. Navigation actions returned by Lia remained available in voice mode. Both regressions were reproduced before their corrections.
- Microphone denial retained a usable text alternative; reduced motion stopped visual animation.
- Voice layout at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px with 22 px root font had no horizontal overflow; SVG IDs were unique.

Final visual review inspected home, ready, listening and editable-transcript screenshots. Existing backend, API contracts and dependency manifests unchanged. No claim of production privacy/compliance or real provider validation.
